Case Study
Smart Laundry Facility Simulation

You have been tasked to simulate the operations of a smart self-service laundry facility.

The facility has:

    •       6 washing machines
    •       4 dryers
    •       2 payment kiosks
    •       A total of 50 customers will arrive throughout the simulation to do their laundry.


The simulation must coordinate:

    •       Access to washers
    •       Transfer to dryers
    •       Payment processing
    •       Statistics collection

 Appropriate assumptions which complement the requirements should be clearly stated and
implemented accordingly.

Basic Requirements:
    1. Customer Arrival
              o    Each customer is represented by a thread.
              o    Customers arrive randomly every 0–3 seconds.
    2. Washing Stage
              o    Customers must wait if all washing machines are occupied.
              o    Washing time: 4–6 seconds per customer.
    3. Drying Stage
              o    After washing, customers wait for an available dryer.
              o    Drying time: 3–5 seconds per customer.
    4. Payment
              o    After drying, customers proceed to one of 2 payment kiosks.
              o    Payment processing time: 1–2 seconds.
Additional Requirements:

    1. Mutual Exclusion

              o    Access to washing machines, dryers, and payment kiosks must be synchronized.

              o    Use synchronized blocks, ReentrantLock, or Semaphore for safe access.

    2. Error Handling

              o    5% chance a washing machine fails mid-cycle (customer must wait and retry).

              o    5% chance a payment kiosk fails (retry after 2 seconds).

    3. Statistics

              o    Collect:

                        ▪    Total customers served

                        ▪    Average total time per customer

                        ▪    Max concurrent washers and dryers in use

    4. Concurrency

              o    Multiple machines and dryers must operate simultaneously, visible in the output.

    5. Simulation Duration

              o    The simulation should run about 60 seconds in total.

Bonus Requirements:

    1. Congested Scenario

Sample Output

To see what is happening dynamically, you must have output from the entry gates, exit gates, and
payment processing reporting all their major events. Add information about which process/thread
is doing the output. This way, you can see if a process/thread acts for another, which is strictly
forbidden.
